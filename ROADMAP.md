## How to make this a genuine research-level portfolio

For every advanced research project, maintain more than just the notebook:

| Artifact                 | What to include                                             |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `paper-notes.md`       | Research problem, paper summary, and theoretical background |
| `implementation.ipynb` | Your implementation and experiments                         |
| `experiments/`         | Training configurations and experiment results              |
| `evaluation.ipynb`     | Quantitative and qualitative comparisons                    |
| `results/`             | Metrics, charts, and visualizations                         |
| `README.md`            | What you built, how it works, and what you learned          |

For paper reproductions, clearly distinguish your reproduced results from the original paper's reported results. Document differences in datasets, hardware, training budgets, and implementation choices.
